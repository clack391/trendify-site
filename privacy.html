Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

This policy covers the Trendify mobile app: what it collects, why, who helps run it, and how you delete it. Questions go to clack1477@gmail.com.

What Trendify collects

Your account

Your email address. If you sign in with Google, also the name and profile picture link Google shares with the sign-in. The settings you choose: language, countries, niches, keywords to watch and alerts.

Your stories

Every story you make in the app: ideas, outlines, characters, styles, scenes and upload text, and the spoken summary of a story when you ask for one (Hear your story).

Your videos

The YouTube links you add to My videos, with their public numbers (views, likes and comments) and the reviews Trendify writes about them.

Your YouTube channel, only if you connect it

Trendify uses YouTube API Services. When you connect a channel you sign in with Google and allow two read-only permissions. With them Trendify reads:

  • See your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): your channel’s name and ID, and your public uploads with their titles, upload dates, lengths and public numbers.
  • See YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content (yt-analytics.readonly): for each upload, its views, watch time, average view duration and percentage watched, subscribers gained, likes, comments and audience retention, including the same numbers for its first 7 days.

Google gives Trendify a key (a refresh token) to read these. It is kept encrypted in a secure vault on Trendify’s server, used only to read the data above, and never shown in the app or given to anyone. Trendify never uploads, edits, deletes, comments on or likes anything on your channel.

Your phone

If you turn on alerts, your phone’s push notification token. If the installed app hits an error, a crash report with the technical details, your phone’s model and system version, the app version and your Trendify user ID (no name or email).

AI cost records

For each AI request, which feature made it, its size and its cost, for the Spending screen and the app’s budget limits.

How it is used

Only to run the features you see in the app: finding trends, planning stories, reviewing your videos, the “What works on your channel” findings, and alerts. Trendify shows no ads, does not sell your data, and does not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Trendify’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to provide the features above, is not used to develop, improve or train general AI or machine learning models, and is never read by a person unless you ask for help with your own data or the law requires it.

Who helps run Trendify

These services process data for Trendify, each only for its part:

  • Supabase stores the app’s data and runs its server functions and sign-in, on servers in London (UK).
  • Anthropic (Claude) writes the story ideas, outlines, scenes and upload text from your story, and the video reviews and channel findings from your videos’ numbers and analytics.
  • Google: the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API (to read the data above), the Gemini API (to describe the story, style and pacing of public YouTube videos, including your own public uploads, and, only when you tap Hear your story, to write a short summary of that story from its outline and read it aloud; this uses Gemini’s free tier, under which Google may use what it receives to improve its products), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (to deliver alerts to Android phones).
  • OpenAI reads the short summary of a story aloud when you tap Hear your story and Google’s free voices cannot read it that day. It receives only that summary.
  • Expo delivers push notifications and app updates to your phone.
  • Sentry receives crash reports, stored in the EU.

Anthropic, Google and Expo may process data in the United States or other countries, under their own terms. Trendify shares data with anyone else only if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Disconnect your channel in Settings › Your channel. Trendify removes its access at Google and deletes the stored key, your channel’s analytics, the first-week numbers and the channel findings. Your list of videos and their reviews stay until you remove them or delete your account.
  • Remove Trendify’s access at Google at any time in your Google Account’s security settings. Trendify then stops reading your channel and shows Reconnect.
  • Delete your account in Settings › Delete my account. This deletes your account and everything listed above that is yours. AI cost records keep their numbers but no longer link to you.
  • Export your stories at any time from Settings.

Otherwise Trendify keeps your data while you have an account. Sentry deletes crash reports after its standard retention period (90 days at most). Trend data comes from public YouTube videos and is shared by everyone who uses the app; it is not your personal data.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Each person can only reach their own data, and the Google key sits in an encrypted vault that the app itself can never read.

Children

Trendify is for creators and is not directed at children under 13.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors’ IP addresses for security.

Google and YouTube

By connecting your channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s own use of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
