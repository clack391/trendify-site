Turn what’s trending into your next story.

Trendify is a mobile app for YouTube storytellers. It shows which stories are rising on YouTube, then helps you plan yours scene by scene.

What Trendify does

Find the stories that are rising

Trendify watches public story videos on YouTube in the niches and countries you pick, and shows which kinds of stories are growing fastest, with what the top videos have in common. It can send you an alert when a strong new trend appears.

Plan your story, scene by scene

From a trend or your own idea, Trendify writes story ideas, an outline, the characters and a style, then every scene: a picture prompt, a clip prompt and the narration. At the end it writes the upload title, description and tags. You make the pictures and clips in your own tools and upload the video yourself.

Learn what works on your channel

If you choose to connect your YouTube channel, Trendify reads your uploads and their YouTube Analytics (read only), reviews how each video is doing, and finds what works for your audience, like which kinds of stories get the most first-week views. Those findings guide your next ideas.

Your YouTube channel

Connecting a channel is optional. When you connect one, you sign in with Google and allow Trendify two read-only permissions: to see your YouTube channel and its public uploads, and to see their YouTube Analytics reports. Trendify never uploads, edits, deletes, comments on or likes anything on your channel.

You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings › Your channel › Disconnect), which also removes Trendify’s access at Google, or from your Google Account’s third-party connections. The privacy policy says exactly what is read, how it is used and how to delete it.

Trendify uses YouTube API Services. By connecting your channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s use of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Who it’s for

Trendify is in private testing with a small group of invited creators. It is not in an app store yet.
